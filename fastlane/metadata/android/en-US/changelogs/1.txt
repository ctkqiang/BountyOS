Initial release · v0.1.0

A read-only bug bounty console for HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti, and YesWeHack.

Includes:
• Dashboard: earnings, stats, attention items
• Reports: cross-platform list with search and filters
• Triage: inbox for action-required and retesting items
• Activity: one unified timeline across all platforms
• Offline cache, pull-to-refresh, change notifications
• Credentials encrypted via Android Keystore, on device only

No platform data is ever submitted or modified.
